## Track 1: VCF Pre-Filtering Findings
**Source:** `track1_step2_exp01-vcf_prefiltering.ipynb`
*   Applying intrinsic QC filters (GQ>=20, DP>=10, and 'PASS') coupled with a strict Allelic Balance filter (0.30 - 0.70 for heterozygous variants) successfully removes nearly half of the raw variants.
*   **Insight:** The raw VCF contained 5,012,204 variants. After intrinsic QC, **4,428,273** variants survived (583,931 dropped: 271,414 by `FILTER`, 29,727 by GQ < 20, 34,646 by DP < 10, 248,144 by allelic balance; none lacked metrics or were HOM_REF/unknown). Most of the noise is removed by the common-variant subtraction, not by QC.
*   **Filter 2 (Common Variant Subtraction):** The allele-aware subtraction against dbSNP Common (MAF >= 1%) dropped **3,836,941** common variants (86.6% of the QC survivors).
*   **Final Output:** We are left with exactly **591,339 ultra-rare and high-quality variants** (11.8% of the input, saved to `data/track1_step2_rare_variants.vcf.gz`), i.e. 88.2% of the raw VCF is stripped before the annotation phase.
*   **Discrepancy resolved (2026-09-17, macOS re-run of `notebooks/notebook/run_step2_prefilter.py`):** an earlier version of this section recorded 2,645,065 QC survivors / 2,264,773 common / 380,292 rare, and `01-procedure.md` recorded "2.64M survived intrinsic QC". Neither set matched the notebook's own cell output. The re-run reproduces the notebook exactly (4,428,273 -> 591,339; the common-dropped count differs by 7 from the notebook's 3,836,934 because the script counts per-allele keys on multi-allelic sites). **591,339 is the figure to rely on.** Runtime on Apple Silicon (M-series, 10 cores): 1.4 min end-to-end vs 405 s for the notebook's per-variant tabix approach.

## Track 1: Environment & Reference-Data Findings (macOS port)
**Source:** `notebooks/notebook/run_step2_prefilter.py`, `track1_step4_prioritize.py`, `track1_step5_submission.py`; branch `track1_opt01-macos-portable`
*   **snpEff GRCh38.99 cannot be downloaded any more.** The versioned `snpEff.zip` is SnpEff 4.5covid19 (2020-04-15). Its `download GRCh38.99` targets `downloads.sourceforge.net/.../databases/v4_5/`, which does not exist; SourceForge only hosts `v4_3` builds (GRCh38.86, GRCh38.92, GRCh38.p7.RefSeq), and the newer `snpeff.blob.core.windows.net` repository used by SnpEff 5.x no longer resolves. Resolution: build `GRCh38.99` locally from Ensembl release 99 (`Homo_sapiens.GRCh38.99.gtf.gz`, primary-assembly DNA, CDS and peptide FASTA) with `snpEff build -gtf22`. Same gene set as the historical prebuilt database.
*   **Patient data is gated on Hugging Face.** `SageBio/mva-hackathon-2026-data` returns HTTP 401 anonymously; access requires accepting the gated-access form and an `hf auth login` token. FASTQs (~62 GB) are not needed for Track 1 and were not downloaded.
*   **Leaderboard contract confirmed from the Space's public source** (`evaluation.py`, `tabs/submit_track1.py`): only `proband_id = PROBAND01` is accepted; chromosome strings are compared verbatim and the ground-truth fallback uses the `chr` prefix; max 10 rows; F-max is computed **per variant**, so a compound-het row contributes two predictions at its epcr.
*   **HPO coverage of the MVA panel is partial.** With HPO release 2026-09-01, *BUB1B* and *CEP57* are annotated to only 3 of the patient's 8 terms (HP:0001518 Small for gestational age, HP:0002859 Rhabdomyosarcoma, HP:0004322 Short stature), *TRIP13* to 2, and *MAD2L1BP* to none (it is not in `genes_to_phenotype.txt`). Exact term overlap would therefore rank the true genes low; the Step 4 matcher uses ontology-aware Resnik similarity and the MVA panel is checked explicitly regardless of score. HP:0000121 Nephrocalcinosis, HP:0003202 Skeletal muscle atrophy, HP:0001622 Premature birth and HP:0200067 Recurrent spontaneous abortion have no direct match in any MVA gene.
*   **`requests` was missing from `requirements.txt`** although Step 3A imports it; added (with `huggingface_hub` for data download, `alphagenome`, `python-dotenv` and `pytest`).

## Track 1: Annotation Findings (Step 3, macOS run 2026-09-17)
**Source:** `snpeff_annotated.vcf`, `notebooks/notebook/track1_step3a_annotate.py`, `notebooks/notebook/step3b_alphagenome.py`
*   **Only 1,526 of the 591,339 "rare" variants are protein-altering** (202 HIGH, 1,324 MODERATE on canonical transcripts); 99.5 % of what survives Step 2 is MODIFIER (intronic/intergenic). The procedure's estimate of ~10-20k was an order of magnitude high.
*   **MyVariant.info must be queried with `assembly=hg38`.** The original Step 3A queried the default hg19 index with GRCh38 coordinates: 1,492 of 1,526 variants came back "notfound" and the 34 "hits" were different variants that happen to share an hg19 coordinate. With `assembly=hg38`, 1,525/1,526 are found. Any Step 3A output produced before this fix must be discarded.
*   **dbSNP Common (b151, 2018) is a weak blacklist for coding variants.** After Step 2, **1,212 of the 1,526 HIGH/MODERATE variants have gnomAD AF > 1 %** (1,163 above 5 %; e.g. chr1:942451 T>C, gnomAD AF 0.9999 — the reference genome carries the minor allele). Spot-checking 300 of the common SNVs against the local `common_all_20180418.vcf.gz` with tabix found **none of them present at all**, so this is not an allele-matching bug in `run_step2_prefilter.py`: the 2018 file simply lacks them. For indels the gap is near-total (278 of 307 have AF > 1 %; dbSNP normalises them differently). Consequence: gnomAD AF from Step 3A is the effective frequency filter, and Step 5 applies the procedure's 1 % cutoff on it (`--max-af 0.01`, not tighter, per `00-references.md` §4). Replacing dbSNP Common with a gnomAD-based blacklist in Step 2 would be the structural fix.
*   **AlphaGenome Atlas scores SNVs only.** `query_variants` raises `NotImplementedError` for every insertion/deletion (alphagenome 0.9.0). 1,220/1,526 variants received `AVI_PHRED`; 257 are in the genome-wide top 1 % (Phred >= 20), 13 in the top 0.1 %. The dominant attribution modality for coding variants is conservation (`CACTUS_241_WAY`) followed by `ALPHAMISSENSE`. `TARGET_GENE` is not returned by the AVI scorers.
*   **MVA panel, as seen in the Step 3 output (research annotation, not a clinical statement; Steps 4/5 not yet run):** the only panel gene with HIGH/MODERATE rare variants is *BUB1B*, with **two heterozygous variants**:
    *   a **stop-gain** (HIGH), gnomAD genomes AF ~3e-5, CADD 36, AVI_PHRED 33.8 (top modality PROTEIN_TERMINATION), ClinVar **Pathogenic/Likely pathogenic** (single submitter), OMIM 257300 (MVA syndrome 1), allelic balance 0.54;
    *   a **missense** (MODERATE) absent from gnomAD, CADD 24.5, REVEL 0.47, AVI_PHRED 25.6 (top modality ALPHAMISSENSE), no ClinVar record, allelic balance 0.46.
    *   Two heterozygous variants in an autosomal-recessive MVA gene is the compound-heterozygous pattern described for MVA1 in `00-references.md`; phase cannot be determined from a single-sample VCF. No HIGH/MODERATE rare variants in *CEP57*, *TRIP13* or *MAD2L1BP*. Exact coordinates and HGVS live only in the gitignored `track1_step3b_regulatory_annotated.vcf.gz` (repo rule: no extracted patient identifiers in git).
